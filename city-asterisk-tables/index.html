    How this works

    2009–18 tables

    • Final Premier League points for 2009‑10 to 2017‑18, the nine seasons the charges cover.
    • Points deduction subtracts the same number from City every season and re-sorts. Teams level on points keep their original order. That roughly reflects goal difference, which City usually led, but it's an approximation.
    • Expunged removes City from the table and moves everyone below up a place. Other teams keep the points they won against City; a true expunging would also strip those results.
    • "Top four" stands in for Champions League places, which were four in every season covered.

    2026‑27 simulator

    • Each team gets one strength rating. It blends current points per game (shrunk toward the league average, so six games don't overstate form) with the bookmaker win/draw/loss odds for this weekend's matchweek‑7 fixtures.
    • Match outcomes use a Davidson (Bradley‑Terry with draws) model with a home-advantage term. All parameters are fitted to those odds.
    • Matchweek 7 uses the real fixtures. The remaining 31 games are drawn against random opponents, home or away at 50/50, because the full fixture list wasn't available. Each team is simulated independently, so results are a good estimate rather than an exact schedule replay.
    • The deduction is applied to City's final total; their form is assumed unaffected.
