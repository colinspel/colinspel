My Premier League, NFL and NBA models against the betting market, on every pick logged before kickoff (PL and NFL since Oct 29, NBA since Nov 8, 2026). Lower score is better.

Model vs market, live picks

How it's scored. Premier League picks use the ranked probability score (RPS) over home, draw and away. NFL and NBA picks use the Brier score on the home side. Lower is better. The market line comes from bookmaker odds captured when each pick was logged, with the margin removed; "naive" always predicts the historical home/draw/away rates (soccer 44/25/31, NFL 56.5/43.5, NBA 55.7/44.3). The gap is model minus market on games where both exist, with a 90% bootstrap interval (2,000 resamples). If the interval crosses zero, the difference is inside noise.

Why the market is the bar. Betting markets price in injuries, lineups and team news. My models only see past results and a prior from last season, so roughly matching the market is a good result and beating it consistently would be rare. Backtests before going live, each on seasons the model never saw while it was tuned: Premier League v5.1 RPS ; NFL Brier ; NBA Brier . The NFL model was rebuilt for week 10 (rest, starting-QB changes, travel and a rolling home edge), so NFL picks up to week 9 come from the earlier version.
