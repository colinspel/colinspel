My Premier League and NFL models against the betting market, on every pick logged before kickoff since Oct 29, 2026. Lower score is better.

Model vs market, live picks

How it's scored. Premier League picks use the ranked probability score (RPS) over home, draw and away. NFL picks use the Brier score on the home side. Lower is better. The market line comes from bookmaker odds captured when each pick was logged, with the margin removed; "naive" always predicts the historical home/draw/away rates (soccer 44/25/31, NFL 56.5/43.5). The gap is model minus market on games where both exist, with a 90% bootstrap interval (2,000 resamples). If the interval crosses zero, the difference is inside noise.

Why the market is the bar. Betting markets price in injuries, lineups and team news. My models only see past results and a prior from last season, so roughly matching the market is a good result and beating it consistently would be rare.
