How it's scored. Each pick is logged before kickoff. "Hit rate" = the model's most likely outcome happened (draws count for soccer). "Skill" = how much better its probabilities were than a naive home-win-rate guess (Brier score; soccer uses the 3-way RPS, as in the backtests). The white marker on each bar is that model's backtested skill; filling past it means it's beating its own track record. Soccer picks marked BACKDATED (10–12 Oct) were generated on 11 Oct using only data from before the 10 Oct kickoffs — no results from that weekend went in — so they're fair predictions, just not logged in advance like the rest. A handful of games is mostly luck — read it as a scoreboard, not proof. Live scores: ESPN's public scoreboard feed, refreshed every 60 seconds while this page is open.
