# Pressing vs Results

**Does pressing actually win football matches? Not the way most people measure it.**

I analyzed 1,517 matches of event data and 764 team-seasons across Europe's big five leagues, from 2015/16 to 2023/24.

📄 **Full study:** [Pressing_Study.pdf](Pressing_Study.pdf)

## Key findings

- **PPDA looks great at first.** In the 2015/16 Premier League, pressing intensity (PPDA) tracked the table closely (r = −0.65).
- **Then it vanishes.** Across four leagues, once possession is controlled for, PPDA's link to points drops to almost nothing. Teams that keep the ball face fewer passes, which flatters their PPDA.
- **Where you win the ball matters.** Possessions that start with a final-third regain end in a shot about 3× as often as those won deeper.
- **Block height is the repeatable edge.** How high a team defends tracks points every season, even within the same team.
- **Low-press winners usually beat their xG.** Leicester (2015/16) and Girona (2023/24) both finished well above their xG-based expectation, which is hard to repeat.

## What I'd tell a coach or analyst

1. Don't judge a press by PPDA alone
2. Track where you win the ball
3. Block height is the repeatable edge
