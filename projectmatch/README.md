# ProjectMatch

**Matching college students with small businesses for short-term projects.**

Small businesses often need help with a defined, short project (a dashboard, a content calendar, a bookkeeping cleanup) but can't justify a hire. College students want paid, real-world work for their portfolios. ProjectMatch is a two-sided marketplace that connects the two.

🖥️ **App design prototype:** [`ProjectMatchAppDesign.tsx`](ProjectMatchAppDesign.tsx), a clickable React prototype with a student view and a business view.

## Student side

- Discover feed with projects recommended for the student's target career path
- Project details, a data & IP agreement, and one-tap applications
- Application tracking, messaging and notifications
- Premium tier ($9.99/mo): priority matching, résumé optimization, interview prep

## Business side

- Post a project with category-based budget guidance
- Transparent pricing: project budget + 15% platform fee, held until the deliverable is approved
- Optional $25 boost to feature a listing for 7 days
- Applicant review with match scores, plus a Business plan ($99/mo) for multiple active projects

**Built with:** React, Tailwind CSS, lucide-react
