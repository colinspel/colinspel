## How it's graded

Each move is scored out of 7: role fit vs. Gasperini's stated need (0-2), current form and minutes (0-2), health (0-1) and deal feasibility at sensible terms (0-2). It grades the move, not the player's ceiling.

## What's in each dossier

- A pitch diagram showing the slot he fills
- One headline number that carries the case
- The case for and against
- What would change the verdict
- A realistic deal path

## Limits

Built from public stats and transfer reporting as of Oct 19, 2026. No film-based grades: physical and tactical claims come from reporting, not observation. Sources are linked inside each dossier.
