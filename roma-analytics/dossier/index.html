Natural left-footed wing-back in the exact system Gasperini built at Atalanta. Lets Wesley go back to the right, or replaces him outright if he's sold. Roma reportedly have him top of Gasperini's left-flank wish list.

Crosses in his 2025-26 breakout (33 games, 27 starts), the volume Gasperini wants from a wing-back. The catch: only 44 were accurate, a 31% hit rate.

Il caso a favore · For

  • System-native: came through Atalanta's 3-4-2-1. Zero tactical learning curve, which matters for a January signing with no preseason.
  • Creator from wide: 3 assists and 38 key passes last season, including a run of 8 straight games with a key pass.
  • Size on the flank: 6'3" helps defend back-post crosses, an area where Roma just conceded three times from corners against Real Madrid.

Il caso contro · Against

  • Lost his spot: mostly rotation and sub minutes so far this season, plus 109 Conference League minutes.
  • Knee history: medium-grade MCL sprain last April ended his season. No surgery, but it's the first thing a medical will check.
  • Defending 1v1: was overrun by Olise in Atalanta's 6-1 loss to Bayern. One game, but elite wingers are the Champions League test.

Changes my mind if

Atalanta hold at €25m+ for a player they aren't starting. Then Oosterwolde (back from surgery around December, Roma reportedly agreed in principle) becomes the cheaper January play.

Deal path

Loan with an obligation tied to appearances, the same structure Roma agreed for Oosterwolde (€1m loan, €15m obligation). D'Amico came from Atalanta, so the relationship exists. Fallback: Cacciamani, 19, left-footed at Torino, but Torino rejected €10m and want double.
