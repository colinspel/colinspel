# Roma Scout

**A matchday scouting app for AS Roma's 2026/27 season.**

📱 **Live app:** [colinspel.github.io/roma-scout](https://colinspel.github.io/roma-scout/) — works on phone and desktop; add it to your home screen for an app-like experience. Source: [`index.html`](index.html).

## What it does

- **Home:** countdown to the next match, Roma's Serie A position, points and form, plus a cumulative points chart against a 2-points-per-game pace.
- **Season:** every Serie A and Champions League fixture with results, filterable by competition.
- **Table:** the live Serie A table with Champions League, Europe and relegation zones marked.
- **Scout:** a dossier for every opponent, with coach, formation, a threat rating, their last five results, danger men, and a pitch diagram of how Roma's 3-4-2-1 lines up against their shape with a three-point game plan.
- **Scout notes:** a notes box per opponent, saved on your device.

## How it works

- Single self-contained HTML file, no build step and no API key.
- Live scores, fixtures and standings come from ESPN's public soccer feed and refresh every minute around kickoff.
- Works offline from a built-in calendar and the last saved sync; past results without live data can be entered by hand.
